# Boston University UROP: Fall 2026 Application Requirements

Official-source audit completed **31 July 2026**. This note uses only Boston University/UROP sources and checks the claims in the user's collected information. The relevant next regular cycle is **Fall 2026**.

## Bottom line

- The Fall 2026 student application is due **Friday, 11 September 2026, by 12:00 PM (noon)**. The faculty recommendation is due **by midnight the same day**. BU says deadlines are subject to change and applies a strict late-work policy. ([Deadlines](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/deadlines/))
- As of 31 July, the Fall form is **not open**; BU says it will open in **early August**, without publishing an exact opening date. ([Application](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-2/))
- The current UROP calendar lists a **Fall 2026 Application Tips session on Tuesday, 25 August at 3:30 PM** and offers application-review appointments. ([Calendar](https://www.bu.edu/urop/current-participants/calendar/))
- Because Prof. Kulis has agreed to mentor the project, the student has the required mentor relationship. The UROP application is needed only to seek UROP funding; volunteer, course-credit, work-study, or faculty-funded arrangements do not use this funding application. Calling the mentor agreement “the hardest requirement” is reasonable advice, not an official BU rule. ([Application Tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/), [Working with Students](https://www.bu.edu/urop/mentors/working-with-students/))

## Eligibility

For Fall stipend funding, the applicant must:

- be a **full-time BU undergraduate**;
- conduct the project under a **BU faculty member**;
- not be on leave;
- not receive both academic credit and a UROP stipend for the same research;
- not already have received UROP funding twice. The live page states that a student funded twice cannot reapply. ([Funding & Eligibility](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/funding-eligibility/), [FAQ](https://www.bu.edu/urop/about/frequently-asked-questions/))

Funding is competitive. A faculty mentor may have at most **two funded students per application cycle**. ([Working with Students](https://www.bu.edu/urop/mentors/working-with-students/))

The live eligibility page places its international-student location rule under a “Summer 2026” heading; it does **not** publish a separate Fall 2026 international-student rule. International applicants who need work-authorization certainty should ask UROP rather than extrapolate the summer statement. ([Funding & Eligibility](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/funding-eligibility/))

## Submission process and mentor requirements

A complete funding application has two separately submitted online components:

1. the student application;
2. a confidential recommendation from the BU faculty mentor. ([Application](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-2/), [Application Tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/))

The mentor must:

- review the student's application **at least once** before submission;
- submit the online recommendation by midnight on 11 September;
- provide half of an award if the student requests a Faculty Matching Grant;
- supervise the work. ([Working with Students](https://www.bu.edu/urop/mentors/working-with-students/), [Funding & Eligibility](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/funding-eligibility/))

BU's 2026 tips say strong recommendations are commonly about one page or three substantive paragraphs, but this is guidance rather than a published hard limit. ([2026 UROP Application Tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2026/02/2026-UROP-Application-Tips.pdf))

### Mentor-notification conflict

Two current BU pages directly disagree:

- the [Application page](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-2/) says the system will notify the mentor after the student applies;
- the [Application Tips page](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/) says the system does **not** notify or direct the mentor.

Do not rely on an automatic message. Send Prof. Kulis the Kerberos-protected recommendation-form link directly and confirm submission before midnight.

## Fall funding and pay rules

The regular Fall application offers student stipend awards, not a project-expense budget:

| Funding request | Approximate commitment | Maximum semester stipend | Funding source |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Student Research Award (SRA) | 5 hours/week | Up to $750 | UROP pays 100% |
| Faculty Matching Grant (FMG) | 5 hours/week | Up to $750 | UROP 50%; mentor 50% |
| Faculty Matching Grant (FMG) | 8 hours/week | Up to $1,200 | UROP 50%; mentor 50% |
| Faculty Matching Grant (FMG) | 10 hours/week | Up to $1,500 | UROP 50%; mentor 50% |

The live funding page identifies SRA as fully UROP-funded, FMG as half UROP/half mentor, and 8- or 10-hour requests as FMG-only. The valid **5-hour FMG** option is also reflected in BU's official Fall announcement; it was omitted from the user's table. ([Funding & Eligibility](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/funding-eligibility/), [BU Office of Research Fall stipend description](https://www.bu.edu/research/2025/08/12/open-for-applications-undergraduate-research-opportunities-program-urop/))

Pay rules:

- awards are taxable, weekly salaried payments through Student Employment;
- Fall awards are paid over **10 weeks**;
- the hourly rate reflects the current state minimum wage;
- total on-campus employment may not exceed **20 hours/week** during the academic year;
- the student cannot be paid twice for the same work hours;
- other funding for the project must be discussed with the mentor. ([Funding & Eligibility](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/funding-eligibility/), [Important Information](https://www.bu.edu/urop/current-participants/important-information/))

BU currently says it no longer provides separate travel or supplies awards, notwithstanding an outdated mentor page that still mentions them. ([Travel & Supplies Awards](https://www.bu.edu/urop/opportunities/travel-awards/), [Working with Students](https://www.bu.edu/urop/mentors/working-with-students/))

## Application sections, prompts, and limits

### What is confirmed on the live website

All applications must include:

- applicant information;
- project title;
- project description and goals;
- project significance;
- methodology;
- timeline;
- background experience;
- bibliography;
- funds requested;
- safety training. ([Application Tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/))

Conditional sections cover continued funding, an appendix, and human- or animal-subject documentation. The form uses **character limits**, including spaces and punctuation, not word limits. ([Application Tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/))

### Limits in the most recent public blank sample

The Fall 2026 form is not yet public. The current tips page links a [blank sample updated March 2025](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2025/03/Sample-UROP-Application-updated-3.2025.pdf-1.pdf) and explicitly warns that it is not the official application. It shows:

| Field | Public-sample limit | Prompt in substance |
|---|---:|---|
| Project title | 500 characters | Concise project title |
| Project description and goals | 3,000 | Lay-accessible background, overall objective, semester goals, and the student's individual role |
| Project significance/importance | 3,000 | Field-level benefit, broader implications, and possible applications—not primarily personal benefit |
| Methodology/process | 3,000 | Specific steps, resources, and processes used to achieve the goals |
| Timeline | 3,000 | Detailed, student-specific plan for the funding period, organized by week or phase |
| Background experience | 3,000 | Relevant research, coursework, skills, mentor relationship, and academic/career connection |
| Bibliography | 3,000 | Sources consulted or informing the project; in-text citations are safer because footnotes do not copy |
| Prior-project results and new goals, if previously funded | 1,500 | Results and completion of prior goals, plus new goals |
| Changes from the previous funded project, if applicable | 1,500 | Mentor, scope, or project changes |

The sample and [2026 tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2026/02/2026-UROP-Application-Tips.pdf) recommend **at least five references**; five is a recommendation, not a stated submission threshold.

The exact Fall 2026 applicant-information, funds-requested, and safety-training subprompts and their limits are not publicly disclosed. Recheck every prompt and counter when the live form opens.

## Required and excluded attachments

For an ordinary project with no regulated human or animal research, BU does not list a résumé, CV, transcript, cover letter, full prospectus, or extra recommendation as a required student attachment. The linked sample says those documents will not be included in review. ([Sample application](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2025/03/Sample-UROP-Application-updated-3.2025.pdf-1.pdf))

Conditional attachments:

- **Human subjects:** IRB approval or an official IRB exemption determination, as applicable.
- **Animal use:** IACUC approval or an official IACUC exemption determination, as applicable.
- **Appendix:** optional; may contain relevant figures, data visualizations, or overflow bibliography, but may not continue over-limit narrative responses.
- **Mentor recommendation:** required, but submitted separately by the mentor rather than attached by the student. ([Application Tips](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/), [Sample application](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2025/03/Sample-UROP-Application-updated-3.2025.pdf-1.pdf))

The sample requests PDF compliance documents with prescribed filenames, but the exact Fall 2026 upload rules must be checked in the live form.

### Attachment-rule conflict

The live [Application Tips page](https://www.bu.edu/urop/get-involved/application-tips/) says the optional appendix may be two pages. The linked 2025 [sample form](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2025/03/Sample-UROP-Application-updated-3.2025.pdf-1.pdf) says one page. Until the Fall form opens, the upcoming-cycle limit is unresolved. The simplest safe choice is to avoid an appendix unless it materially improves the application.

## Human/animal compliance

The current sample says neither UROP nor the faculty mentor can determine whether IRB or IACUC review is required; the appropriate compliance office must make that determination. Work cannot begin until required approval or exemption is obtained. ([Sample application](https://www.bu.edu/urop/files/2025/03/Sample-UROP-Application-updated-3.2025.pdf-1.pdf), [BU Human Subjects Forms](https://www.bu.edu/research/ethics-compliance/human-subjects/supplemental-forms/))

The collected statement that existing NTU data plus synthetic corruption “likely” avoids human-subject review may be a sensible hypothesis, but it is **not an official determination**. Confirm it through Prof. Kulis and the appropriate BU compliance office.

## Official-source discrepancies to resolve when the form opens

1. **Mentor notification:** automatic according to the Application page; not automatic according to Application Tips.
2. **Appendix length:** two pages on Application Tips; one page in the linked sample.
3. **Lifetime funding cap:** two awards on the current eligibility and FAQ pages; three in the older sample. Treat the live **two-award cap** as controlling unless UROP says otherwise.
4. **Fall 2026 field limits and upload rules:** only the 2025 sample is public on 31 July.
5. **Exact Fall payment dates:** BU confirms a 10-week period but has not posted Fall 2026 start/end or pay dates.

## Practical pre-submission checklist

1. When the form opens, verify every field limit, funding choice, and appendix/compliance upload rule against the live Fall 2026 form.
2. Confirm with Prof. Kulis: SRA versus FMG, 5/8/10 weekly hours, matching funds, and the 10-week scope.
3. Draft the application in the separate fields; make the individual contribution explicit and write for non-specialist reviewers.
4. Obtain any official IRB/IACUC determination required by BU.
5. Have Prof. Kulis review the complete form draft at least once.
6. Use the 25 August tips session and/or schedule an application-review appointment before deadline day.
7. Submit before noon on 11 September and independently verify the mentor recommendation before midnight.

## Post-award forms (not application attachments)

If funded, BU separately requires an Award Acknowledgment Form, Press Release Permission Form, Intellectual Property Agreement, Mentor Approval Form, and—if not already on file—employment forms I-9 and W-4. These are acceptance/employment steps, not materials to attach to the application. ([Important Information](https://www.bu.edu/urop/current-participants/important-information/))
