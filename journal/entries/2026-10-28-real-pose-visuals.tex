\entry{28 October}{Showing the input checks with a real movement}{Local rendering of retained development evidence; no new scientific run.}

The researcher requested four synchronized views of a real development pose,
motion traces, a short video, a journal figure and a LinkedIn progress draft.
The earlier communication figures used synthetic movement. This version uses
the retained clean and corrupted arrays from the accepted 11 October geometry
audit, job 8028081, while preserving their historical execution-r1 identity.

The action was chosen as sitting down before inspecting the pose arrays or any
model scores. Within that action, the example is the first sorted identifier in
the existing fixed base panel. The source summary matched its documented hash;
its panel, verification receipt, archive and all 430 tensor hashes also matched.
The selected identifier was confirmed in the development-validation manifest.
Only the retained geometry files were copied read-only from SCC. The original
aggregate was not loaded and the cluster job hold was not resumed.

The four views show the uncorrupted input, coordinate jitter, missing joint
trajectories and missing frames. The video replays the same 100 model frames
at each of the three fixed strengths. A right-ankle trace under each view
connects the skeleton to a measured coordinate. Confidence-zero observations
remain absent, including whole masked frames; the clean reference trace is
identified separately. Display speed does not imply the original capture rate.
These are 2D pose estimates, and ``clean'' does not mean ground truth.
