\entry{28 October}{Showing the input checks with a real movement}{Local rendering of retained development evidence; no new scientific run.}

The researcher requested four synchronized views of a real development pose,
motion traces, a short video, a journal figure and a LinkedIn progress draft.
The earlier communication figures used synthetic movement. This version uses
the retained clean and corrupted arrays from the accepted 11 October geometry
audit, job 8028081, while preserving their historical execution-r1 identity.

The action was chosen as sitting down before inspecting the pose arrays or any
model scores. Within that action, the example is the first sorted identifier in
the existing fixed base panel. The source summary matched its documented hash;
its panel, verification receipt, archive and all 430 tensor hashes also matched.
The selected identifier was confirmed in the development-validation manifest.
Only the retained geometry files were copied read-only from SCC. The original
aggregate was not loaded and the cluster job hold was not resumed.

The four views show the uncorrupted input, coordinate jitter, missing joint
trajectories and missing frames. The video replays the same 100 model frames
at each of the three fixed strengths. A right-ankle trace under each view
connects the skeleton to a measured coordinate. Confidence-zero observations
remain absent, including whole masked frames; the clean reference trace is
identified separately. Display speed does not imply the original capture rate.
These are 2D pose estimates, and ``clean'' does not mean ground truth.

This render explains the checks without supplying a new scientific result.
The historical arrays do not replace the fresh runtime acceptance still
required before selection. The storage hold and the sealed final test remain
unchanged. The LinkedIn text separates the accessible task explanation from
the technical training comparison; nothing was posted. Public attachment
rights for the real-data media remain unestablished, so the media and source
arrays stay outside Git.

The researcher then requested a more legible, less generic social version.
The revised 22-second film uses a portrait layout with a large opening pose,
four synchronized panels in a two-by-two arrangement, and a brief explanation
of the current input-checking stage. Monochrome, Helvetica Neue and precise
dividers follow the existing project design direction. Solid observations,
dashed original references and a ring on the tracked ankle distinguish the
data without adding a decorative color scheme. The post now starts with an
everyday explanation and follows with the frozen-encoder and full-recipe
comparison, keeping pending work separate from results.

The encoded film was inspected at phone width and all 550 frames decoded
without errors. Text contrast was measured; the lowest text/background ratio
was 5.74:1. Review confirmed shared frame indices and coordinate limits,
confidence-based masking and unfilled trace gaps. Source hashes and the
original journal figure hashes remained unchanged. The social renderer,
copy-ready post, alternative text and evidence-backed design review are
retained beside the original deliverables. No new scientific computation,
cluster access or social publication was performed for this revision.

The researcher subsequently confirmed the intended LinkedIn use and selected
the original wide video. Its first two headline lines were removed by cropping
only the top 144 pixels. The resulting 1920-by-936 film retains all 360 frames
and the original 18-second timing, with the panel labels and traces intact.
The post was shortened while retaining the accessible and technical
explanations. Full decoding and a visual check passed. Nothing was posted;
the final local attachment is \path{output/real-pose-progress/linkedin-wide.mp4}.
